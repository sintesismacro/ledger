# The Guild

**Status: the Arena opens with the ledger's first questions** (planned for December 2026), once
counsel has cleared the contributor terms. The terms are published here before it opens.

## The Arena and the Guild

- **The Arena** is open to anyone. You forecast the same open questions as the founder, under the
  same schema, the same rules in [`METHOD.md`](METHOD.md) and the same scoring, by submitting a
  structured issue form under a GitHub handle of your choice.
- **The Guild** is the people the Arena collects by the quality of their judgement, not by their
  fame. A forecaster joins the Guild wall once ten of their forecasts have resolved, ranked by
  Brier score with the number of resolved forecasts shown beside it.

## Rules

- You set your own probabilities. No one else may write them (METHOD.md § 3).
- A probability is locked when it is recorded; a changed view is a new forecast (METHOD.md § 4).
- The same perimeter applies to everyone: no named issuer or security, and no performance claims.
- You appear only under the handle you choose. No other personal data is published.
- Contributor terms will state how a member can leave. The working proposal is that the handle is
  removed and the forecasts stay, anonymised, because a ledger with gaps proves nothing. The
  final terms follow counsel's advice.
