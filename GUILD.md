# The Guild

**Status: closed.** The Guild opens to invited forecasters once the ledger has 20 resolved
forecasts, and once counsel has cleared the contributor terms.

## What it will be

A small group of people collected by the quality of their judgement, not by their fame.
Members submit forecasts by pull request, under the same schema, the same rules in
[`METHOD.md`](METHOD.md) and the same scoring. Their scores appear alongside the founder's.

## Rules that will apply

- Membership is by invitation, and you appear only under a GitHub handle you choose.
- You set your own probabilities. No one else may write them (METHOD.md § 3).
- The same perimeter applies to everyone: no named issuer or security, and no performance claims.
- A personal leaderboard row appears after 10 resolved forecasts.
- Contributor terms will state how a member can leave. The working proposal is that the handle is
  removed and the forecasts stay, anonymised, because a ledger with gaps proves nothing. The
  final terms follow counsel's advice.
