# Letter 0 — Why this ledger exists

Berlin, 24 October 2026

Intelligence is becoming abundant. Every week it is cheaper to produce an analysis, a model, a
forecast, a confident paragraph. When something becomes cheap, something else becomes scarce. My
bet is that the scarce thing is judgement that has been checked.

This ledger is where I check mine, in public.

## What it is

One file per forecast. Each has a question that resolves on a date, the source that decides it,
the rule for reading that source, and a probability. Before the number, each file carries two
voices.

- **Becoming**, after Spinoza: intelligence extends as a body — chips, power, grid, cooling,
  machines — and that body keeps growing.
- **Returning**, after Mainländer: every necessity removed consumes something, and the build-out
  moves towards its own exhaustion.

The probability is where I think the two meet. The score will show whether I was right.

## What it is not

No forecast names a company or a security, and nothing here is investment advice. The questions
are about the physical world — electricity, interconnection queues, semiconductors, robots — and
about how much of what is published on AI in finance survives replication.

## How it is kept honest

- Agents draft. Only I write a probability, in a commit of my own, and CI checks that it is mine.
- Locked fields never change. A changed view is a new file, and the misses stay.
- Every resolved forecast is scored with the Brier rule against a permanent 50%. The calibration
  chart appears once ten forecasts have resolved.
- Each commitment will carry a qualified electronic timestamp, so its date is proved, not claimed.

## What comes next

The first forecasts are planned for December 2026: ten to begin with, twenty by the end of the
year, about half of them resolving within four months so that a first score exists early. The
Atlas — the physical layers of the build-out — fills as forecasts open.

The Arena opens with the first questions. Anyone can forecast the same questions under the same
rules, under a handle of their choosing. Those whose scores hold up become the Guild. Until then,
the [Observatory](https://sintesismacro.github.io/observatory/) has a calibration trainer built
on fourteen questions that have already resolved.

## Who this is for

People who would rather be calibrated than impressive. If you can show me why one of my
probabilities is wrong before its resolution date, I want to hear it — in an issue, with a source.

From Q1 2027 a letter like this one is merged every quarter, so that the diff shows which beliefs
moved, by how much and on what evidence.

What SINTESIS does for funds is what this ledger does for me: it checks a research claim
independently, on a method fixed in advance, before anyone relies on it.

FREDY SOLANO
Founder and CEO, SINTESIS
