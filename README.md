# Forward Evidence Ledger

**Intelligence becomes abundant; verified judgement becomes scarce.**

When intelligence is cheap, claims are cheap. What stays scarce is judgement that has been
checked. This ledger checks ours, in public. Every forecast is dated, sourced and scored, and the
misses stay in.

## Becoming vs Returning

Every forecast carries two voices before it carries a number.

- **Becoming**, after Spinoza: *the order and connection of ideas is the same as the order and
  connection of things.* Intelligence extends as a body: fabrication, power, grid, cooling,
  actuators. The physical layer is where it is priced first.
- **Returning**, after Mainländer: every necessity removed consumes something. The build-out
  moves toward exhaustion, and every edge toward its own extinction.

The probability is where the two meet. The score shows who was right.

## What is forecast

| Domain | The question underneath |
| --- | --- |
| `physical-ai-power` | When does the price of intelligence's body stop rising while the price of intelligence falls? Read from power, grid, semiconductor and robotics indicators. |
| `ai-finance-replication` | How much of what is published about AI in finance survives independent replication? |

## How to read it

- [`forecasts/`](forecasts/) holds one file per forecast. Questions, probabilities and voices are
  locked once merged.
- [`METHOD.md`](METHOD.md) sets out the rules: who sets probabilities, no backfill, proof of time,
  scoring and annulment.
- The `evidence` branch holds the rendered scorecard, calibration chart and Atlas, plus qualified
  timestamp receipts for every forecast commitment.
- [`letters/`](letters/) holds the quarterly letters, each of which shows how beliefs moved.
